# Railway & Metro Construction — Questions I Cannot Answer

Answer format: value · unit · route / line · category · source · date

· 1.2 [GATE] What triggers aborted or curtailed possessions on your projects — late handover, weather, isolation failure, protection failure, train running late — and what each costs you.
· 1.3 [GATE] Your possession overrun penalty and the point at which an overrun becomes a reportable event on your network.
· 1.4 [GATE] What a line closure or temporary speed restriction costs your operator — or the operator's stated figure — per hour or per day.
· 1.5 [GATE] Your access cost set — site access/walk-in and setup, protection setup (lookouts / COSS / TOWS), isolation setup and confirmation, clear-site and handback, track access charge per possession, rail labour premium over civils, night shift premium, standby plant charged but not worked, on-track machine per shift, RRV access point setup, aborted possession per event.

· 4.8 [GATE] How rail expansion is accounted for at fixed points on your routes — bridges, S&C, level crossings, structures — as your design states.
