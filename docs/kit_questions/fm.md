# Facility Management — Questions I Cannot Answer

Answer format: value · unit · quality band · market · source · date · confirmed or indicative

Section 1 — The asset register

For each asset field — unique ID, location to room level, make/model/serial, installation date, criticality, warranty status/expiry, statutory obligation, PPM regime/frequency, expected life/replacement year, replacement cost, spares held, O&M manual reference, commissioning record:

· Do you hold it? Source of truth? How verified?
· 1.1 How was the register built — O&M handover, physical survey, or accumulated over time? What percentage do you believe is accurate?
· 1.2 When was it last physically verified against the building, and what did that verification find?
· 1.3 What is your criticality rating scheme, and what does each level actually change — response time, spares held, redundancy, PPM frequency?
· 1.4 Which assets are not in the register and why? Tenant installations, landlord additions, temporary equipment that became permanent.
· 1.5 Which assets in the register no longer exist? How would you find out?

Section 2 — Statutory and compliance

For each obligation — fire alarm, extinguishers, sprinkler/suppression, emergency lighting, fire doors, fire risk assessment, water hygiene/legionella, water tanks, lifts/escalators, pressure systems, lifting equipment, fixed wiring, PAT, gas safety, asbestos, air quality/ventilation, generator load test, local authority/civil defence:

· Frequency? Who may perform? Certificate produced? Consequence of lapse? Regulation?
· 2.1 Which carry personal liability for a named individual, and who is that person?
· 2.2 Which certificates does an insurer or authority ask for first when something happens?
· 2.3 What happens when a statutory item is overdue — grace period, or non-compliant from the day after?
· 2.4 Which statutory items are most often missed, and why?

Section 3 — SLA and performance

· 3.1 Define precisely as your contract uses them — response time, attendance time, rectification/fix time, temporary repair/make safe, permanent repair, resolution. For each: clock starts? clock stops?
· 3.2 SLA matrix for P1/P2/P3/P4: definition, response, rectification, deduction if missed.
· 3.3 Who decides priority of a call, and what happens when the client disagrees?
· 3.4 Which KPIs are you actually measured on, and which can be met while the building performs badly?
· 3.5 What stops the clock legitimately — parts lead time, access denied, third-party dependency? How is that evidenced?

Section 4 — Cost

For each item — hard services per m²/year, soft services per m²/year, reactive spend %, PPM spend %, out-of-hours callout, specialist subcontractor visit, emergency plant hire, consumables/parts, energy, lifecycle/capital replacement fund:

· Unit? Low? Mid? High? Source?
· 4.1 What is your reactive-to-planned ratio, and what is the target? What does a rising reactive percentage actually mean?
· 4.2 What does deferred maintenance cost when it eventually lands? Give a real example.
· 4.3 What is the cost of downtime by asset type — lift out, chiller down, power loss, water loss? Who bears it?
· 4.4 Which asset failure has the worst ratio of prevention cost to consequence cost?

Section 5 — The three regimes

· 5.1 For every maintenance activity: which regime — statutory, contractual, manufacturer warranty, insurer requirement, discretionary/good practice? Can it be deferred? Who authorises deferral? Consequence?
· 5.2 Which assets have warranty conditions voided by missing a service, non-OEM parts, or wrong contractor? Who tracks that?
· 5.3 When budget is cut mid-year, what is the order in which things get dropped, and who signs that off?
· 5.4 Has anything ever been deferred that turned out to be statutory? How was it caught?

Section 6 — Handover and the liability gap

· 6.1 Map overlapping regimes on a newly handed-over building — defects liability period, manufacturer warranty, installer/subcontractor warranty, FM maintenance obligation, insurance. For each: duration? who pays for a failure? who does the work?
· 6.2 When something fails in year one, how do you determine whether it is a defect, warranty item, maintenance failure, or fair wear? Who decides, and what happens when contested?
· 6.3 What did you not receive at handover that you needed — O&M manuals, as-builts, commissioning records, spares, training, software licences, access credentials, BMS passwords?
· 6.4 How long after handover before you actually understand the building? What accelerates it?
· 6.5 What should FM have been consulted on during design that it never is?

· 7.5 What is the handover between shifts and between contractors, and what has been lost across it?
